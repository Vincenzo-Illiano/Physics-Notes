In this topic we will use [[Fermi-Dirac and Einstein-Bose distributions|Fermi-Dirac distribution]] to study the behaviour of fermions in a gas when the condition 
$$
\frac{V}{v_{Q}} \ll N
$$
is not valid. These gases are known as **degenerate Fermi gases**. An example are electrons in a metal. In addition, we will assume that the fermions in the gas are free particles. This is in general not true, especially for electrons in metals, where the interaction with the lattice is not negligible.

- [[Fermi gases at zero temperature]]: We will calculate the average energy and pressure in the assumption that ${}T = 0{}$.
- [[Density of states]]: We will generalize the results at ${}T > 0{}$ by using a concept known as **density of states**.
- [[Sommerfeld Expansion]]: We will use a series expansion to get approximate results.
