**Boltzmann statistics** is the branch of statistical physics developed by Ludwig Boltzmann in the eighty hundreds. Boltzmann statistics, and in particular the [[Canonical ensemble]], enable us to study complicated models without having to explicitly compute the multeplicity ${}\Omega{}$.

## Topics

- [[Canonical ensemble]]: we describe the Canonical ensemble and derive the probability to find a system in a specific microstate when in thermal equilibrium with a large resevoir
- [[Partition function]]: we describe some useful properties of the partition function ${}Z{}$ and how it can be used to determine other statistical quantities
- [[Equipartition theorem]]: the equipartition theorem is stated and proved. 
- [[Applications of Boltzmann statistics]]: different applications of Boltzmann statistics are provided, most notably the [[Ising model - Boltzmann statistics|Ising model for paramagnets]] and the [[Ideal gas - Boltzmann statistics|Ideal gas]].
