# Boltzmann and Gibbs statistics

**Boltzmann statistics:** 
$$ 
P(s) = \frac{1}{z} e^{-\beta E(s)}, \qquad \beta=\frac{1}{kT} 
$$
**Partition function:**
$$
Z = \sum_{s}e^{-\beta E(s)}
$$
**Average values:**
$$
\bar{X} = \frac{1}{Z} \sum_{s} X(s)e^{-\beta E(s)}
$$
**Average energy:**
$$
\bar{E}=-\frac{1}{Z} \frac{ \partial Z }{ \partial \beta }, \quad \bar{E^{2}}=\frac{1}{Z}\frac{ \partial^{2} Z }{ \partial \beta^{2} }
$$
