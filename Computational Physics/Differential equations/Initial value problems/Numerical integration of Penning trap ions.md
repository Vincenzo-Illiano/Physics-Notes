The equations derived from eq. (1) in [[Penning trap|The Penning trap]] form coupled [[Initial value problems]]. [[Runge-Kutta methods|RK4]] can integrate the position and velocity together; the magnetic force depends on velocity, so the elementary velocity-independent Verlet formulas do not apply directly.
