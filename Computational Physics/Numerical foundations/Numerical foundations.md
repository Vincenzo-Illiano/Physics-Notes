# Numerical foundations

Parent: [[Computational physics]]

A numerical calculation replaces continuous mathematical objects and exact arithmetic with finite representations. Its reliability depends on both the approximation made by the algorithm and the arithmetic used to implement it.

- [[Binary representation]] — integers and floating point formats.
- [[Floating point errors and FLOPs]] — round-off, truncation error and operation counts.
- [[Discretization and numerical differentiation]] — grids, finite differences and Taylor expansions.
- [[Equation scaling]] — dimensionless variables and natural scales.
