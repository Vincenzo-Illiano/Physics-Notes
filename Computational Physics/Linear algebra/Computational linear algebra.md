# Computational linear algebra

Parent: [[Computational physics]]

Discretized physical problems often become either linear systems, $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$, or eigenvalue problems, $A\mathbf{v}=\lambda\mathbf{v}$. Matrix structure helps determine an efficient solution method.

- [[Matrix equations]] — Gaussian elimination and the Thomas algorithm for tridiagonal systems.
- [[LU decomposition]] — factorization and its uses for solving systems, determinants and inverses.
- [[Iterative methods]] — Jacobi, Gauss–Seidel and successive over-relaxation.
- [[Jacobi rotation method]] — eigenvalues and eigenvectors of real symmetric matrices.
