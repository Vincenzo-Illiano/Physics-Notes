## The model

The **two-dimensional Ising model**, also called the **Lenz–Ising model**, describes a square lattice of spins $s_i\in\{-1,+1\}$. Each spin interacts with its nearest neighbours horizontally and vertically, not diagonally. We consider ferromagnetic coupling (so two parallel spins decrease the total energy of the system) and no external magnetic field.

The system is in thermal contact with a bath at temperature $T$. Thermal fluctuations allow spin configurations to change. Increasing temperature makes energetically unfavourable fluctuations less strongly suppressed; a particular spin-flip probability also depends on the energy change and the chosen dynamics or sampling algorithm.
