In this topic, we will introduce a series of corrections to the hydrogen atom orbitals and energy levels, in order to determine what is referred as **hydrogen fine structure**. The reason why this topic is studied is that the hydrogen atom is one of the simplest real scenarios where quantum mechanics can be applied and experimentally tested. 

We will in particular look at the following corrections:
- [[Relativistic correction - Hydrogen atom |Relativistic correction]]: to account for a relativistic term in the kinetic energy.
- [[Spin-Orbit interaction - Hydrogen atom |Spin-orbit interaction]]: To account for the interaction between the magnetic dipole generated by the electron due to its spin and the proton.
- [[Darwin term - Hydrogen atom|Darwin term]]: To account for *zitterbewegung*, an effect which can be derived from the Dirac equation.
